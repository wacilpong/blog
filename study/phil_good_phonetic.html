1. Vowel Sounds (Monophthongs & Diphthongs)

Vowels are voiced sounds produced with an open vocal tract. (shape of mouth)

Category Phonetic Symbols & Target Words
Short Vowels Produced quickly without glide
Long Vowels Elongated sound duration (:)
Diphthongs Gliding from one vowel to another

2. Consonant Sounds (Voiced & Unvoiced)

Consonants are articulated by obstructing the flow of air. (Using teeth, tongue, lips)

Category Phonetic Symbols & Target Words
Unvoiced Consonants Vocal cords do not vibrate
Voiced Consonants Vocal cords vibrate during sound
